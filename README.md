## 👋 Hey, I'm Darshan H V

<p align="center">
  <i>A curious mind who enjoys understanding technology and turning ideas into reality.</i>
</p>

I'm someone who enjoys taking an idea and discovering how far it can go.

My interest in technology goes beyond learning programming languages or using existing tools. I enjoy understanding how things work internally, exploring the concepts behind them, experimenting with different approaches, and finding ways to turn those ideas into something practical.

I'm particularly interested in the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Software Engineering, System Architecture, Networking, Automation, and Real-World Engineering**.

What excites me most is seeing how these areas can work together. A machine-learning model can become part of a larger application. Software can interact with physical hardware. Engineering calculations can become automated workflows. Different technologies can come together to solve a problem that would otherwise require several separate processes.

That's the kind of work I enjoy exploring.

## ✨ A Little More About Me

<table align="center" border="0" cellpadding="8">
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 💡 Curiosity is where I start

Most of my ideas begin with a question. What if an existing process could be automated? What if different technologies could work together? What happens behind the scenes when an application communicates with another device?

I don't always start with a complete solution. Sometimes a rough concept is enough to push me into unfamiliar territory.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🔍 Research before implementation

I enjoy the investigation before development — reading documentation, comparing approaches, understanding limitations, and exploring existing implementations.

Understanding **why** an architecture or technology works makes implementation far more meaningful.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td valign="top">

### 🧪 Experiment, observe, improve

I enjoy testing models, exploring local LLMs, trying fine-tuning techniques, evaluating model behaviour, experimenting with communication protocols, and comparing architectures.

Not every experiment works, but every experiment teaches me something.

</td>
<td valign="top">

### 🏗️ Building complete systems

I enjoy connecting frontend, backend, APIs, databases, hardware, and intelligent models.

For me, building a system isn't just about making individual features work — it's about making **all the pieces work together**.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td valign="top">

### ⚙️ Engineering meets software

I enjoy translating real-world engineering concepts into software through calculations, algorithms, visualization, design rules, and automation.

I like projects where different technical disciplines meet.

</td>
<td valign="top">

### 🔧 Understanding why things break

Debugging is an important part of how I learn. I prefer tracing the actual cause of a model issue, database problem, network failure, or performance bottleneck instead of only patching the symptom.

</td>
</tr>
</table>

  <img src="https://readme-typing-svg.demolab.com?font=Fira+Code&weight=600&size=17&duration=2800&pause=900&color=FFB020&center=true&vCenter=true&width=780&height=40&lines=Still+learning.+Still+experimenting.+Still+building.;What+if%3F+%E2%86%92+How+does+it+work%3F+%E2%86%92+Let+me+build+it." alt="Building philosophy"/>
</p>

I want to keep developing the ability to take an idea from its earliest concept through research, design, implementation, testing, and improvement.

My goal is not simply to collect programming languages or frameworks. It's to understand problems across different technical layers, choose appropriate tools, design a solution, and build systems that are genuinely useful.

I'm still learning, experimenting, making mistakes, and discovering new possibilities — and that's exactly what makes this journey exciting.

> **I enjoy asking "What if?", figuring out "How does it work?", and eventually building "What if I make it myself?"**
